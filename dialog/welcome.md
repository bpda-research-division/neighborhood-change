Please note that this site is best viewed from a laptop or desktop computer.

### First time here?

To explore data for specific places within Boston, try:
* clicking on one or more areas on the map
* to un-select an area, click on it again
* the **Clear selections** button in the map un-selects all areas
* click the **MBTA lines** button to toggle a transit overlay

When you select more than one area, use the **Combine / Compare** toggle to choose how the data are shown:

* **Combine** adds the selected areas together and displays them as a single series
* **Compare** keeps each selected area as its own series, shown side-by-side on the bar chart and as separate lines on the line chart (up to 5 areas can be compared at once)

To explore change over time, try:
* clicking and dragging the time slider
* pressing the &#9658; button on the time slider
* clicking on a different year on the line chart

To view population projections by neighborhood, click the Neighborhoods tab and toggle projections on for the **Population**, **Age**, or **Children by Age** topics.

To download data, use the **Download data** button located at the bottom right of the map.
